Why we exist

A more inclusive
world for
dyslexic minds.

Too often, the conversation around dyslexia focuses on what is difficult — reading, writing, spelling or keeping up — rather than the person behind those challenges.

We are here to change that. Brilliantly Dyslexic raises awareness, provides trusted information and connects people to support, resources and opportunities.
