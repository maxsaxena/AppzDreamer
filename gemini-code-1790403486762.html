The Parent Journey

A journey, not a label.

Clear answers at every step of your child's journey. Finding out your child may have dyslexia can bring a lot of questions. We help families move:

Understanding → Assessment → Support → Strengths → Possibilities

What is dyslexia?

Dyslexia is a difference in how the brain processes language. It can affect reading, spelling and writing, while many children also have strong abilities in areas such as creativity, problem-solving and visual thinking.

Could my child be experiencing it?

Every child is different. Some may struggle with reading or spelling, while others may find organisation, memory or processing information more difficult.

What does assessment involve?

An assessment looks at how your child learns, where they may need support and where their strengths lie — helping you understand what they need to thrive.

What support is available?

From classroom strategies and assistive technology to specialist support, the right tools can make learning more accessible and help children feel more confident.

Changing the World Around Them

It takes all of us.

Building confidence starts with understanding. Children thrive when the people around them understand how they learn — at home, in school and eventually in the workplace. We work to build greater awareness and acceptance of dyslexia across:

Families

Practical guidance that helps parents support their children.

Schools & Educators

Better understanding and more inclusive learning environments.

Corporates

Awareness and inclusion that extends into skills, careers and employment.

Government & Policy

Advocacy for systems that recognise and support different ways of learning.
